# Kredit audio Couple Party

Semua audio berlisensi **CC0 1.0 (domain publik)** — bebas dipakai tanpa syarat. Kredit di bawah ini sukarela, sebagai terima kasih.

| Dipakai untuk | Judul | Pembuat | Sumber |
|---|---|---|---|
| Musik menu (`music/menu.ogg`) | Funked Up | Joth | https://opengameart.org/content/funked-up |
| Musik UNO (`music/uno.ogg`, `music/uno-tense.ogg`) | Funky Menu Loop | iamoneabe | https://opengameart.org/content/funky-menu-loop |
| Musik Ular Tangga (`music/snakes.ogg`, `music/snakes-tense.ogg`) | Happy Clappy Loop | OwlishMedia | https://opengameart.org/content/happy-clappy-loop |
| Suara kartu (`sfx/card-*`, `pack-*`, `chips-stack`, `dice-shake`) | Casino Audio | Kenney (kenney.nl) | https://opengameart.org/content/54-casino-sound-effects-cards-dice-chips |
| Suara tombol & giliran (`sfx/ui-*`, `turn-ding`, `switch`) | Interface Sounds | Kenney (kenney.nl) | https://opengameart.org/content/interface-sounds |
| Jingle saksofon (`sfx/jingle-*`) | Music Jingles | Kenney (kenney.nl) | https://opengameart.org/content/85-short-music-jingles |

Pengolahan (potong, loop mulus, normalisasi loudness, OGG) dilakukan dengan `scripts/audio/build.py`.
